A simple ritual

Ten quiet minutes.

Incense has been part of meditation across Asia for centuries. The practice is small: a flame, a little smoke, and your attention.

  1. Light the charcoal

    Light a charcoal disc and wait until it turns grey at the edges.

  2. Set it down

    Place it on a bed of ash in a heat-safe burner.

  3. Add a pinch

    Put a small pinch of powder on the charcoal, or on a mica plate above it.

  4. Sit and breathe

    Let the smoke settle. Snuff the charcoal when you are done.

Burn in a ventilated room. Keep charcoal and burner away from children, pets and anything flammable, and never leave them unattended.
